## Subagents

- Spawn subagents freely to save tokens: hand searches, file sweeps, mechanical edits and other routine work to a cheaper model than the one you are running (e.g. `model: "haiku"` or `"sonnet"` on the Agent tool) without asking first.
- Keep design decisions and final review in the main session; give the subagent a self-contained prompt and only relay what matters.
